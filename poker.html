Equity and ranges

Your share of the pot

Equity is how often your hand wins if the cards ran out from here, found by simulating thousands of run-outs. Players who raise usually hold strong hands, so the main number weights each opponent by their actions: raisers get the top fifth of hands, callers the top half. The figure against random hands is shown for comparison.

Pot odds

Price versus chance

If you must call $20 into a $60 pot, you put in 20 to win 80, so you need at least 25% equity to break even. When your equity beats the price, calling makes money over time even when it loses this hand. The EV line shows the average result of the call.

Outs

Cards that save you

Outs are unseen cards that improve you to a likely winner, such as the nine remaining hearts for a flush draw. A quick rule: multiply outs by 4 on the flop (two cards to come) or by 2 on the turn for a rough chance to hit. The panel lists the exact cards.
