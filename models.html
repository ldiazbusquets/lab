Encoders and decoders

Two kinds of model

BERT and FinBERT are encoders: they read the whole sentence at once and turn it into a judgement. Qwen and Llama are decoders: they predict the next word, over and over, which is what lets them write. Module 06 builds a tiny decoder from scratch.

Why fine-tuning matters

Same brain, different training

FinBERT is BERT with a few thousand labelled financial sentences added on top. That is enough to learn that “costs fell” is good news and “guidance cut” is bad, which general BERT often gets backwards. Try the restructuring headline.

Why the chatbots ramble

Small models, real limits

Qwen3 and Llama here are hundreds of times smaller than ChatGPT or Claude, squeezed to 4-bit numbers so they fit in your browser. They sound fluent but can be confidently wrong, which is exactly what module 14 is about.
