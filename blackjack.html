Expected value

Every move has a price

For each option, the panel works out the average result per dollar bet, using the exact cards still left in the shoe and the dealer's possible outcomes. Positive means you profit on average; the best move is the least negative one, even when every option loses.

Basic strategy

The mathematically best play

The chart is the strategy that maximises expected value for these rules. Played perfectly, it cuts the house edge to roughly half a percent. The Coach flags any move that differs from it, and the session panel tracks how closely you follow it.

Card counting

When the shoe turns in your favour

High cards favour the player, low cards favour the dealer. The Hi-Lo count tracks the balance: +1 for 2 to 6, −1 for tens and aces. Divided by the decks left, the true count says when to bet more and when a few plays change. Counting in your head is legal, but casinos can ask counters to leave.
