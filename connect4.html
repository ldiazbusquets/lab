Solved in 1988

The first player wins

James D. Allen and Victor Allis independently proved that with perfect play, the player who starts wins, but only by dropping the first disc in the middle column. Starting one column off-centre draws; starting further out loses.

How the solver works

Search to the end

Once a dozen or so discs are down, the engine searches every line to the end of the game and labels each column win, draw or loss. Earlier, there are too many lines to finish in time, so it shows a scored estimate from a shallower search: open threes, open twos and the centre column.

Reading the scores

Bigger means faster

Solved columns show W, D or L with a number: a higher number wins sooner (or, for a loss, holds out longer). The coach flags any move that turns a win into a draw or loss, or a draw into a loss.
