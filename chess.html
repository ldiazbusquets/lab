The engine

Search, then judge

The engine looks ahead through every legal reply, then replies to those, pruning lines that cannot matter (alpha-beta search). At the end of each line it scores the position: material, plus where each piece stands. Higher levels search deeper and stop adding deliberate mistakes.

The evaluation

Scored in pawns

+1.00 means White is ahead by about a pawn. A knight or bishop is worth about 3, a rook 5, a queen 9. “M3” means a forced checkmate in three moves. The bar beside the board shows the same score from White’s side.

The coach

How each move is graded

Before you move, the engine scores every legal option. Your move is compared with the best one: within 0.1 pawns is best, 0.5 good, 1.2 an inaccuracy, 3 a mistake, and anything worse a blunder. Accuracy summarises how close you stayed.
