The cards

Cards decide the turn

  • 1, 2 or 3: make that many moves in a row.
  • Skip: make one move, then your opponent loses their turn.
  • Reverse: switch sides for one move and move one of their pieces.
  • Wild: choose any effect and the next colour.

Matching

Follow the pile

Play a card that matches the colour or the symbol on top of the discard pile. Wilds always play. With no match, draw one card. If it still does not match, you get a single plain move. Everyone holds three cards and refills after each turn.

Winning

Take the king

There is no checkmate here: you may leave your king in check, and if your opponent gets a move, they can capture it. Capturing the king wins. The King safety panel warns you when a one-, two- or three-move turn could reach your king.
