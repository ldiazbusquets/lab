About

Why this exists

I study finance at Bryant University, with minors in Applied AI and Law. I built the Lab to understand these models well enough to build them myself, and to test where they help with real financial decisions and where they do not.

Each agent page reports what the agent got wrong, not just what it got right, and compares it with simple rules a person could follow.

Built withPlain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks.
ModelsYOLOX-tiny and a face detector on ONNX Runtime Web; every other model is written from scratch.
DataSimulated markets, leagues and portfolios; licensed stock footage for the feed.
PrivacyNo cookies, no tracking. Settings stay in your browser.
Educational only. Every market, trading, betting and portfolio result on this site uses simulated data. Nothing here is investment advice.
