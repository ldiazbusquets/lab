How the odds are found

Every arrangement that fits

Each number says exactly how many mines touch it. The solver lists every way of placing mines along the edge of the cleared area that agrees with all the numbers, then weights each one by how many ways the leftover mines can sit in the untouched squares. A square's probability is the share of arrangements with a mine there.

Sure things

0% and 100%

When a square is a mine in every arrangement, it is certain; when it is clear in every one, it is safe to dig. Most of Minesweeper is spotting these, and the panel counts how many the current position proves.

Forced guesses

When logic runs out

Some positions have no certain square. Then the best move is the lowest probability, often an inner square away from the numbers. Even a perfect solver loses a share of Expert games to these guesses, which the solver record shows.
